FAQ

Clear answers before you start.

Does Earnr guarantee I’ll make money?

No. Earnr helps you compare and execute realistic opportunities, but actual results depend on your market, effort, consistency, decisions, and many factors outside the app.

Why only three initial recommendations?

Because a short, differentiated set makes comparison easier. Earnr intentionally avoids dumping dozens of generic ideas on you.

What is the Opportunity Score?

A structured 0–100 evaluation covering personal fit, demand, competition, ease, speed to first money, startup-cost fit, scalability, and execution risk.

What happens after I pick a path?

Your choice becomes your active business and Earnr builds the execution plan around that exact business instead of switching you to something else.

How much is Premium?

Premium is $10 per month. No free trial is being advertised right now, and you can manage or cancel the subscription through the app.
